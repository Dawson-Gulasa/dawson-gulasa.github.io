About my work

From schematic to working system.

Growing up around computers led me to care increasingly about the electronics and design processes that make them possible. I gravitated toward the space where physical hardware and software meet.

My work spans high-speed PCB layout, digital logic and RTL, embedded programming, electronics, verification and physical prototyping.
